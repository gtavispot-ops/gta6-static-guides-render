GTA 6 Characters Guide

Full profiles of Lucia Caminos, Jason Duval and the supporting cast shaping Rockstar's Leonida crime saga.

A Story Told Through Two Outlaws

For the first time in the series, Grand Theft Auto VI centers on a criminal duo whose partnership — romantic and professional — drives the entire narrative. Rockstar has described the story in Bonnie-and-Clyde terms: two people against a corrupt state, bound together by loyalty and desperation. For detailed dossiers on every named face revealed so far, see the GTA 6 characters guide, which tracks each reveal as new trailers land.

Lucia Caminos

Lucia is the series' first female lead protagonist, and the trailers make her the emotional core of the story. Fresh out of a Leonida prison, she is determined to build a life on her own terms — but the odds are stacked against her, and old habits pull hard. Sharp, resourceful and unwilling to be underestimated, Lucia's arc appears to explore what happens when the system fails someone twice. Expect her missions to blend high-stakes heists with the struggle to stay free in a state that profits from keeping people locked up.

Jason Duval

Jason wants the easy life: sun, money, no complications. Instead he works odd jobs for a local drug runner and keeps getting dragged into schemes bigger than himself. Where Lucia is hardened by experience, Jason is the dreamer of the pair — loyal to a fault and dangerous when cornered. The second trailer frames much of the story from his perspective, suggesting players will spend significant time in his shoes as the duo's plans spiral across Leonida.

The Supporting Cast

Leonida is crowded with scene-stealers glimpsed across both trailers. Brian Heder is the aging drug runner Jason works for — a Keys-era smuggler clinging to a fading empire. Cal Hampton, Jason's conspiracy-obsessed friend, provides comic relief with his shoreline paranoia. Boobie Ike is a Vice City legend who converted street hustle into a legitimate empire of clubs, studios and real estate — the kind of kingpin who hires people like Lucia and Jason. Dre'Quan Priest, a music hustler trying to break into the industry, and Raul Bautista, a seasoned bank robber always hunting the next big score, round out the criminal ecosystem. Around them orbit influencers, corrupt officials and reality-show caricatures that satirize modern Florida.

How Dual Protagonists Change the Game

GTA V's three-way character switching was a landmark feature, and GTA 6 refines it around a tighter duo. Expect seamless switching between Lucia and Jason in free roam, with certain missions playable from either perspective and others locked to one character for story reasons. The relationship system may also matter mechanically — choices made as one protagonist could affect missions, dialogue and opportunities available to the other, raising the stakes of the partnership beyond cutscenes.

What the Cast Tells Us About the Story

The mix of small-time hustlers and entrenched kingpins suggests a classic GTA rise through the ranks — from convenience-store robberies to scores that shake the state. Boobie Ike's legitimate-front empire hints at missions straddling the music and club scenes, while Raul Bautista's crew points to the big multi-stage heists the series is famous for. And with law enforcement, rival crews and federal attention all circling, the Bonnie-and-Clyde framing promises a story where trust is the scarcest currency in Leonida.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who are the GTA 6 protagonists?
Lucia Caminos and Jason Duval, a criminal duo at the center of a Bonnie-and-Clyde-inspired story set in Leonida.

Can you switch between characters?
Rockstar hasn't detailed the system, but the dual-protagonist setup strongly suggests a refined version of GTA V's character switching.

Who is the main villain?
No primary antagonist has been confirmed. Candidates include entrenched kingpins like Boobie Ike or forces within law enforcement — see the GTA 6 characters breakdown for the latest theories.
