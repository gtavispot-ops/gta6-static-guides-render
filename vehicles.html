GTA 6 Vehicles Guide

Cars, bikes, boats and planes spotted in the trailers — and how vehicle ownership is expected to work across Leonida.

The Biggest Vehicle Roster Yet

Vehicles have always been the beating heart of Grand Theft Auto, and GTA 6 looks set to deliver the largest and most varied lineup in series history. The trailers showcase everything from bouncing lowriders on Ocean Drive to fan boats skimming swamp water, monster trucks crushing mud pits, and jumbo jets roaring over Vice City International. For the most complete catalog of confirmed rides, check the GTA 6 vehicles guide, which tracks every car, bike, boat and aircraft revealed so far.

Cars: From Exotics to Beat-Up Sedans

Leonida's roads are packed with automotive variety. Trailers have revealed sleek supercars and sports coupes for Vice City's wealthy districts, classic American muscle cars, lifted pickup trucks, lowriders with hydraulic suspensions, and plenty of worn-out sedans that fit the state's seedier corners. Expect the familiar mix of stealable street traffic and high-end machines purchased from in-game dealerships or websites, mirroring the economy systems of GTA V and GTA Online.

Motorcycles, ATVs and Off-Road Machines

Two-wheeled transport gets plenty of screen time: sports bikes weaving through downtown traffic, choppers cruising the beachfront, dirt bikes tearing through rural trails, and quad bikes built for the wetlands. Monster trucks and dune buggies also appear, suggesting expanded off-road gameplay in Leonida's countryside — mud-racing events and backwoods exploration look like a real possibility.

Boats, Airboats and Seaplanes

With water everywhere — from the Atlantic coastline to the swampy interior — boats matter more in GTA 6 than in any previous entry. Speedboats, yachts, jet skis and flat-bottomed fan boats (perfect for the Everglades-style marshes) have all been spotted, alongside seaplanes that can land on both runways and open water. Smuggling missions by sea and air feel like a natural fit for the story's criminal economy.

Aircraft: Helicopters and Planes

Helicopters buzz over the Vice City skyline in the trailers, from news choppers to police air support, while stunt planes and private jets hint at purchasable aircraft for wealthy players. Given the map's scale, air travel may be the fastest way to cross Leonida — and aerial heists could become a signature mission type.

Buying, Stealing and Storing Vehicles

The classic GTA formula should return: steal anything with wheels (or a hull, or wings) off the street, or buy premium vehicles from dealerships and in-game websites. Expect personal vehicles to be stored in garages tied to safehouses, with insurance and tracking systems — likely evolved from GTA Online — protecting your prized rides. Watch for a return of the car-impound system for vehicles you abandon mid-chaos.

Customization

Vehicle mod shops in the spirit of Los Santos Customs are almost certain to return. Trailers show heavily customized rides — lifted suspensions, neon underglow, custom paint and body kits — so expect deep visual and performance upgrades: engine tuning, armor, turbo, wheels, liveries and more. Building a signature car for Lucia or Jason will be half the fun.

Tips for Vehicle Hunters

Rare spawns tend to appear in specific districts, so learn the map: plan your scouting routes with the GTA 6 interactive map to find dealerships, mod shops and rumored rare-vehicle locations. Grab a fast bike early for getaways, keep a sturdy 4x4 for swamp runs, and always insure the cars you love — Leonida is not kind to the unprepared.

While Rockstar hasn't detailed the system, trailers show extensively modified vehicles, and customization has been a series staple since GTA: San Andreas.

Helicopters, stunt planes and seaplanes all appear in the trailers, so expect a full range of aircraft.
