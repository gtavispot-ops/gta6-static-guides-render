Welcome to Leonida: GTA 6 Returns to Vice City

Grand Theft Auto VI takes players back to Vice City, this time reimagined for the modern era inside the sprawling fictional state of Leonida — Rockstar's take on Florida. Neon-soaked Ocean Drive streets, sun-bleached suburbs, swampy Everglades-style wetlands and island chains inspired by the Keys all combine into what is expected to be the largest and most detailed open world the studio has ever built. If you cruised the 1980s version of Vice City, this homecoming has been more than two decades in the making.

Rockstar has confirmed a dual-protagonist story in the spirit of a modern Bonnie-and-Clyde tale. Lucia Caminos — the series' first female lead protagonist — is fresh out of prison and determined never to go back, while her partner Jason Duval wants a quiet life but keeps getting pulled deeper into Leonida's criminal underworld. Their relationship sits at the heart of the narrative, and players will reportedly switch between the two characters much like the three-protagonist system that defined GTA V.

Release Date and Platforms

Rockstar has announced that Grand Theft Auto VI is coming to PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. A PC version has not been officially confirmed, but the series' history strongly suggests one will follow after the console launch — as happened with GTA V and Red Dead Redemption 2. The release window has shifted since the game's first reveal, so check a dedicated news tracker for the currently announced date before making plans.

Why Vice City Matters

Vice City is hallowed ground for GTA fans. The 2002 classic defined an era of open-world gaming with its pastel-noir style, unforgettable soundtrack and rags-to-riches crime saga. Returning to that setting with modern technology — dense crowds, dynamic weather, wildlife-filled swamps and a living social-media-style in-game culture parodying real Florida headlines — gives Rockstar a chance to deliver both nostalgia and something entirely new. Trailers have leaned into the state's stranger-than-fiction reputation, from alligator-wrestling side content to chaotic convenience-store robberies.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is GTA 6 set in Vice City?
Yes. The game is set in the fictional state of Leonida, with Vice City as its centerpiece, alongside surrounding countryside, wetlands and islands.

Who are the main characters?
Lucia Caminos and Jason Duval, a criminal duo whose partnership drives the story. See our characters guide for full profiles.

Which platforms will GTA 6 launch on?
PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S have been confirmed. PC details remain unannounced.
